# Product Vision — Restaurant Ordering and Status Platform

**Status:** Draft  
**Restaurant context:** One independent casual-dining restaurant

## Vision Statement

For the owner-operator and staff of an independent casual-dining restaurant who need to coordinate service without relying on paper tickets, verbal updates, and separate manual records, the Restaurant Ordering and Status Platform is a standalone restaurant-operations application that gives guests and staff one current view of the menu, orders, and service progress.

Guests can confidently browse the current menu before speaking with a server. Servers can record what each table ordered, coordinate with the kitchen, deliver food, and finish service with an accurate bill. Kitchen staff can work from the same order record, communicate readiness or exceptions, and keep availability current. Administrators can maintain the restaurant information that makes those workflows dependable.

Unlike disconnected menus, paper tickets, and verbal handoffs, the platform keeps the information needed during service aligned across the people who use it. The result is fewer avoidable ordering and availability mistakes, less manual coordination, and clearer completion signals for food and service.

## Business Buyer and Value

The owner-operator is the business buyer. They decide whether to adopt, pay for, and retain the platform. The product is worth keeping when it reduces avoidable mistakes, shortens new-server onboarding, and makes service more consistent without creating more maintenance work than it saves. The owner-operator may stop using it if staff work around it, menu and table changes do not reach everyone, or the operating benefit does not outweigh the product’s cost and upkeep effort.
