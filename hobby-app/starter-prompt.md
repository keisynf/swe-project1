# Starter prompt

Paste as the first message in each model session. Nothing attached.

Rewrite any phrasing that does not sound like you before you use it. You are answerable for the
final text, and the idea reads better when the wording is yours.

---

```
I want to build an app for discovering new hobbies. I like starting new hobbies, but it is hard
to know where to start, and what I am looking for changes: sometimes I want something quick,
cheap, and low effort, and other times I want something physically demanding. Finding out what a
hobby will actually require is slow and scattered, so there is no easy way to match a hobby to
what I am up for right now. Every hobby in the app would show what it demands across four
dimensions, mental, physical, monetary, and time, in a form you can filter and compare across
hobbies instead of researching them one at a time. Once you pick one, the app tells you where to
go to get started: what to buy, specifically enough to act on, such as crochet hooks in sizes 4,
5, and 6 and particular yarn weights, and where to learn, such as pointing you to look for videos on
basic crochet stitches.

Act as the product manager for this product and write a draft product vision statement, using
Geoffrey Moore's template from Crossing the Chasm (FOR, WHO, THE product is a, THAT, UNLIKE,
OUR PRODUCT). The product has no name yet, so use [PRODUCT] as a placeholder. Keep it short, a
vision is a statement and not a specification. Then list separately the assumptions you had to
make, the questions you would ask me to sharpen it, and anything you think is weak about the
idea.
```
